1-Month Breakdown

How the month runs

Sparse programming: a few reviews and checkpoints, with large uninterrupted build blocks.

  1. Week 1

    Lock the subnet thesis, scoring signal, and incentive design with technical review.

  2. Week 2

    Build the miner/validator loop, repo scaffolding, and a minimal end-to-end benchmark.

  3. Week 3

    Iterate scoring, run simulations, harden against gaming, and tune throughput/quality.

  4. Week 4

    Deployment plan, docs, demo, and an operator-ready rollout roadmap.
